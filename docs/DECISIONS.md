- 2026-09-30 — Task 2.1 choices: (1) New York-close D1/H4 bars are **computed on demand** from H1 (`data/resample.py`), not saved, so raw broker files stay the only stored record and there is nothing to keep in sync. (2) Each bar is labelled by its **start** time (MT5 convention) and also carries `end_utc`, `trading_day`, `h1_bars` and `partial`; a bar with fewer H1 bars than a full day (23) or block (3/4) is `partial` (holiday, early close, data hole, data edge), and missing days get no bar at all (holes are never bridged). (3) The resampled spread is the median H1 spread **rounded up**, because a bar's recorded spread is usually its minimum. (4) ATR uses **Wilder's smoothing** (the standard definition) and was built in 2.1 instead of 2.3 because `resample-check` compares ATRs. (5) Market-hours rules moved from `validation.py` to `data/market_hours.py` unchanged, so validation and backtests share one definition.
