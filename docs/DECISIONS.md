- 2026-09-30 — Task 2.5 engine design (`backtest/engine.py`): (1) For strategies above M5, open trades and pending orders are **managed on the M5 bars inside each decision bar** (the strategy still decides only on its own timeframe). This one mechanism gives the approved SL/TP tie rule (M5 settles it), the exact Friday 16:30 close, and the D1/H4 proposal: a market entry right after a reopen **waits for the first M5 bar after the 15-minute window** inside the same bar (for M5 strategies there is no later sub-bar, so the entry is skipped and counted).
